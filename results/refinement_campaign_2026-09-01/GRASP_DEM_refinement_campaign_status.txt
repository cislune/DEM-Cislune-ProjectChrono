GRASP DEM REFINEMENT CAMPAIGN
Launch date: 2026-09-01 Pacific

CURRENT STATE

The first 8 mm campaign is running unattended on the Cislune Dell RTX A4000.
The complete baseline pilot passed preflight, reference-spin, output-integrity,
mobility-extraction, and chained-state checks. Absolute compaction remains withheld
because the shared 8 mm bed reaches only 80.5 percent of measured UCF out-track density.

STAGE 1 SCOPE

- 150 Alabama-wheel cases: 15 ten-lap sequences.
- Laps 1-5 remain the calibration partition; laps 6-10 remain held out.
- Wheel-soil friction refinement: 0.75, 0.825, 0.90, 0.975, and 1.05.
- One-factor sensitivity: particle friction, rolling resistance, Young's modulus,
  restitution, and integration timestep.
- 60 candidate cases: ten sequential Alabama load/speed/slip conditions for each of
  smooth control, broad wave, chevron, two low-grouser variants, and staggered wave.
- Every candidate starts from the same frozen bed realization and is normalized to
  smooth control. Alabama physical torque is not treated as a validation target for
  non-Alabama wheel geometries.

AUTOMATIC SAFEGUARDS

- Every case has a 1,200 second wall-time cap.
- Completed cases are skipped on restart, allowing safe resume after interruption.
- The campaign stops before available disk space falls below 30 GB.
- A failure in one case is recorded and does not discard completed work.
- Result summaries are regenerated after each completed ten-case block.

NEXT ADAPTIVE STAGE

After Stage 1, retain the baseline material parameters and select wheel friction using
calibration laps only. Use held-out performance, timestep sensitivity, and candidate
rank robustness to choose the next expensive runs. The next campaign will use multiple
bed seeds and a 6 mm particle-resolution bridge, concentrating on smooth control and the
best two candidate geometries. Additional UCF testing should be requested only after
these runs identify the smallest physical data set that can resolve the remaining model
uncertainty.
