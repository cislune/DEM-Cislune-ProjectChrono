GRASP DEM WHEEL CALIBRATION AND VALIDATION
Run date: 2026-09-01

DECISION

Use wheel-soil friction 0.90 for the current 8 mm pilot model. Print and test the
broad-wave candidate beside the smooth control. Do not advance the chevron candidate
from this screen. Continue using DEM torque and normalized geometry comparisons, but
withhold absolute compaction predictions until the terrain bed is density matched at
finer particle resolution.

PHYSICAL EVIDENCE USED

- UCF Alabama RIDER, 10 laps, 2026-08-04: measured load, speed, torque, and derived slip.
- UCF Alabama CPT, 2026-08-13: out-track baseline and post-traffic in-track spatial contrast.
- Historical RTGS RIDER campaigns: Closed Sharp, Closed SIU, and Closed Scalloped wheels.
- Exact Alabama and controlled CRATR collision meshes plus smooth, broad-wave, and chevron OBJ files.

TORQUE CALIBRATION

Raw Alabama torque is not a valid DEM target by itself. The raw torque channel has a
large direction-dependent stationary offset near 3 N m. Each lap was corrected by its
same-direction, loaded-stationary median. The corrected residual still includes dynamic
rig and drivetrain losses and is therefore an upper bound on wheel-soil contact torque.

Calibration used laps 1-5 only. At frozen wheel-soil friction 0.90, the five-lap median
DEM contact torque was 0.913 N m versus the 0.880 N m corrected RIDER upper bound, a
3.7 percent error.

HELD-OUT VALIDATION

Laps 6-10 were not used to select friction. Their median DEM torque was 0.928 N m versus
0.960 N m observed, a 3.4 percent error. Four of five laps were within 20 percent. Lap 10
was a retained outlier at 2.873 N m predicted versus 1.13 N m observed. The torque result
is classified PASS_MEDIAN_WITH_OUTLIER, not an unqualified pass.

COMPACTION LIMITATION

The 8 mm shared terrain bed achieved only 80.5 percent of the measured UCF out-track
bulk density, so it fails the density gate. The chained DEM column-density proxy reached
1.278 after ten passes versus the UCF in-track/out-track bulk-density ratio of 1.140.
UCF q100 increased 7.734 times, but the present wheel run does not simulate an in-track
CPT and cannot claim that resistance ratio. Compaction results are directional and
comparative only until a finer density-matched bed is run.

HISTORICAL RTGS CHECK

The uncalibrated historical current channel gives physical demand order Sharp > SIU >
Scalloped, while DEM torque gives Scalloped > SIU > Sharp. This strict motor-demand
ordering check failed. Current units and tare are undocumented and should not be used for
future calibration without the controller record.

Using known-unit mobility evidence, physical slip ranks Sharp > SIU > Scalloped and DEM
drawbar efficiency ranks Sharp > Scalloped > SIU. Two of three pairwise relationships are
correct, and both methods select Sharp as the best of the historical group.

FROZEN CANDIDATE SCREEN

Relative to the smooth control under identical frozen conditions:

- Broad wave: 1.287x settlement, 1.293x column strain, 1.040x torque, 0.867x drawbar efficiency.
- Chevron: 1.131x settlement, 1.132x column strain, 1.439x torque, 0.445x drawbar efficiency.

Broad wave is the best compaction/mobility trade in this bounded screen. Smooth is the
required control. Chevron is dominated by broad wave on settlement, torque, and drawbar.
